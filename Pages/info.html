Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about FreshMart grocery delivery

Delivery & Shipping

What are your delivery areas and times?

We deliver across Colombo, Kandy, and Gampaha districts. Delivery slots are available from 8 AM to 10 PM daily. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 2 PM.

What is the minimum order for free delivery?

Free delivery is available for all orders above LKR 3,000. For orders below this amount, a delivery fee of LKR 250 applies.

Can I schedule delivery for a specific time?

Yes! During checkout, you can select your preferred 2-hour delivery window. We'll send SMS updates about your delivery status.

Products & Quality

How fresh are your fruits and vegetables?

We source directly from local farms and suppliers daily. Our cold chain logistics ensure freshness from farm to your doorstep. All produce is quality checked before dispatch.

Do you offer organic products?

Yes! We have a dedicated organic section with certified organic fruits, vegetables, and pantry items. Look for the green "Organic" badge on product pages.

What if I receive damaged or expired products?

Contact us immediately through the app or call center. We'll arrange a replacement or refund within 24 hours. Please keep the original packaging for verification.

Payments & Accounts

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and mobile wallets (Genie, FriMi, mCash). All online payments are secured with SSL encryption.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

You can modify or cancel orders within 1 hour of placement. After this, orders enter processing. Contact our customer service for urgent changes.

How do I create a FreshMart account?

Download our app or visit our website. Click "Sign Up" and provide your mobile number. We'll send a verification code to complete registration.

Terms & Conditions

Please read these terms carefully before using FreshMart services

Account Registration

To use FreshMart services, you must register an account with accurate and complete information. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

Age Requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and place orders. Users under 18 must have parental consent and supervision.

Account Security

Notify us immediately of any unauthorized access to your account. We reserve the right to suspend accounts showing suspicious activity.

Order Placement

All orders are subject to product availability. We reserve the right to limit quantities and refuse service at our discretion.

Pricing

Prices are subject to change without notice. While we strive for accuracy, pricing errors may occur. We reserve the right to cancel orders containing pricing errors.

Product Substitutions

If a selected item is unavailable, we may substitute with a similar product of equal or greater value. You can refuse substitutions upon delivery.

Delivery Windows

We deliver within selected time slots. While we strive for punctuality, delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed.

Failed Deliveries

If delivery fails due to incorrect address or recipient unavailability, additional delivery charges may apply for rescheduling.

Delivery Acceptance

Please inspect items upon delivery. Report any issues immediately to our delivery personnel or customer service.

Payment Methods

We accept various payment methods as listed on our website. All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways.

Payment Security

Your payment information is encrypted and secure. We do not store complete credit card details on our servers.

Refunds

Refunds are processed according to our Return Policy. Processing times may vary depending on your payment method.
