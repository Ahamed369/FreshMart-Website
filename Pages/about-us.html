How It All Began

FreshMart was born out of a simple observation: university students struggle to access fresh, affordable groceries while balancing their studies and busy schedules.

2021

The Idea

Our founder, Sarah Johnson, was a computer science student who found herself choosing between expensive delivery services or time-consuming grocery trips. She realized there had to be a better way.

2022

First Prototype

Sarah partnered with fellow students Mike Chen and Emily Rodriguez to create the first version of FreshMart, serving their university campus with a simple web app and bicycle delivery.

2023

Expansion

FreshMart expanded to serve 25+ universities across the country, building partnerships with local farms and suppliers to ensure the freshest products at student-friendly prices.

2024

Today

We've served over 100,000 students while maintaining our core mission: making fresh, quality groceries accessible and affordable for student communities.

Our Mission

To empower university students with convenient access to fresh, affordable groceries, enabling them to focus on their studies while maintaining a healthy lifestyle. We believe every student deserves quality nutrition without the hassle or high costs.

Our Vision

To become the leading grocery delivery service for students worldwide, creating a community where healthy eating is accessible, affordable, and convenient for every university student, regardless of their location or budget.
