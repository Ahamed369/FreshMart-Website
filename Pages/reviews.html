Review Guidelines

Writing Helpful Reviews

  • Be specific about your experience with the product
  • Include details about quality, freshness, and value
  • Mention how you used the product
  • Add photos to show the actual product
  • Compare with similar products if possible

What to Avoid

  • Offensive, inappropriate, or hateful language
  • Reviews not based on actual experience
  • Personal information about others
  • Promotional or commercial content
  • Reviews about delivery or customer service (use feedback form instead)

Rating Guidelines

5 Stars: Excellent - Exceeded expectations in all aspects
4 Stars: Very Good - Met expectations with minor issues
3 Stars: Average - Met basic expectations
2 Stars: Poor - Below expectations with significant issues
1 Star: Very Poor - Completely unsatisfactory

Photo Guidelines

  • Upload clear, well-lit photos
  • Show the actual product you received
  • Include multiple angles if helpful
  • Show any issues or defects clearly
  • Maximum 5 photos per review
